Werewolves vs Villagers rules

Five to twenty players receive secret roles from the server. At night, living werewolves each cast one private ballot. During the day, every living player casts one private ballot. A player cannot vote for themselves or vote twice in a phase.

When everyone eligible has voted, or the phase ends by server timeout, a single player with the most votes is eliminated. Ties and empty ballots eliminate nobody. Ballot owners and targets remain hidden during play; the eliminated player is announced after resolution.

Villagers win when every werewolf is eliminated. Werewolves win when living werewolves equal or outnumber living villagers. If neither team wins by the end of day twenty, the game is a draw. Roles become public when the game ends.
