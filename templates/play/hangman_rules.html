How to play Hangman

Players take turns choosing one unused letter. Every occurrence of a correct letter appears in the hidden word. An incorrect guess uses one attempt.

The team wins by revealing every letter before the wrong-guess limit. When the limit is reached, the round ends and the answer is shown.

Turns follow seat order. A timed-out turn also uses one attempt. Spectators and OBS overlays see the same masked word as players, and the answer stays hidden until the round ends.
